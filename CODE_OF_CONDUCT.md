# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

Contributors and maintainers must keep participation welcoming, respectful,
and focused on evidence. Acceptable behavior includes constructive feedback,
clear disagreement about inclusion criteria, respect for different experience
levels, and correction of mistakes without personal attacks.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained
disruption, publishing another person's private information, or using project
reviews to attack contributors or listed-project maintainers.

## Editorial Disagreement

Inclusion and removal decisions are about this list's documented scope and
evidence, not a judgment of a contributor's worth. Discuss claims, sources, and
criteria directly. Maintainers may close repetitive or bad-faith arguments
after giving a clear rationale.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately using a contact method listed on the
repository owner's GitHub profile. Do not use a public issue when a report
contains sensitive personal information. The maintainer will review reports
confidentially and may edit or remove content, close discussions, restrict
participation, or ban accounts when proportionate to the behavior.

This policy applies in repository discussions and in public interactions where
someone is representing this project.
